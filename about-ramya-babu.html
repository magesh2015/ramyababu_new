A self-taught UI/UX Designer

who enjoys understanding how digital products work and redesigning them to work better for users

My journey into UI/UX design was inspired by my brother, who is also a Product/UI/UX designer. Through his guidance, I learned core design fundamentals, real-world problem‑solving approaches, and how designers think beyond visuals. His mentorship played a major role in motivating me to pursue UI/UX design seriously and build a strong foundation.

I’ve completed the Google UX Design (Coursera) and continue learning through hands-on exploration, design communities, and real-world product analysis. I focus on combining UX thinking, creativity, and clean visual design to solve user problems with clarity and purpose.

I believe good design should feel natural users shouldn’t have to think about how it works.

Designing with AI, Not Against It

As AI reshapes the design industry, I see it as a tool that enhances not replaces human centered design.  I believe strong UX will become even more important in ensuring products remain intuitive, ethical, and easy to use.

I’m actively adapting by strengthening my problem‑solving skills and learning how designers can collaborate with AI to create meaningful user experiences.
